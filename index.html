Junior Software Engineer

Greetings! Thank you for taking the time to visit my website. I've also gained a lot of development experience by creating & deploying multiple websites, including one for my university's Japanese club, and another for the SACNAS chapter at my university (a diversity-focussed STEM student organization).

Speaking of my university, this may be a good time to mention that I'm a Junior Computer Science major at the University of Houston, with a minor in Mathematics, planning to graduate in May 2024. My major's focus is on software engineering, and the focus of my minor is Data Science & Statistics.

In the future, I hope to continue working on meaningful projects to solve problems and challenge myself, while contributing to positive causes and helping people achieve their goals.
